#  Understanding Operational Roles Before Learning Data 

Before learning Excel, SQL, Power BI, Python, Machine Learning, or AI, it's important to understand where business data actually comes from.

Every report, dashboard, KPI, forecast, and AI model starts with operational activities performed by real people.

This guide breaks down the day-to-day responsibilities of common operational roles and helps you understand the business processes behind the data. Please map out which roles are you in and if something is missing you can simply comment an issue i will update it. 

---

# 🏭 Warehouse Associate

### Primary Responsibility

Move products through the warehouse accurately and efficiently.

### Daily Activities

* Receiving products
* Verifying quantities
* Scanning inventory
* Moving inventory to storage locations
* Picking customer orders
* Packing orders
* Labeling shipments
* Loading trailers
* Performing inventory counts
* Reporting damaged inventory
* Following warehouse procedures

### Systems Commonly Used

* Warehouse Management System (WMS)
* RF Scanner
* ERP System
* Shipping Software

# 🤔 Why This Matters

Most people see these jobs as operational roles.

Businesses see them as data-generating roles.

Every scan.

Every transaction.

Every shipment.

Every inventory adjustment.

Every production record.

Every customer interaction.

Creates data that eventually becomes:

* Excel Reports
* SQL Queries
* Power BI Dashboards
* Forecasting Models
* Machine Learning Models
* AI Systems

Before learning analytics tools, try to connect your daily operation with data. 

The better you understand the operation, the easier it becomes to understand the data.
